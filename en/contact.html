Frequently asked questions

What data do you need from us to start?

Only the boundary of your region or parcels. We source and process the satellite and climate data ourselves.

Can we run a pilot?

Yes. We can run the module you need on a sample area so you can compare results with your own field data.

In what format are outputs delivered?

Web dashboard, API, map files (GeoTIFF, Shapefile) and PDF reports, depending on your needs.

Can farmers use it too?

Yes. Alerts go out as plain SMS, so there is no app to install.
