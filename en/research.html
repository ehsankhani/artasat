Featured paperIran Agricultural Research · 2026Accepted · Articles in Press

An explainable machine learning framework to analyze the influence of environmental drivers on vegetation condition: Applications in agricultural drought assessment

Authors
Amirhossein Haddadi, Behnam Rahimi
Journal
Iran Agricultural Research — Shiraz University
DOI
10.22099/IAR.2026.54487.1718

We studied how precipitation, air temperature, solar radiation and aerosol optical depth (AOD) affect the Vegetation Condition Index (VCI) in the Shushtar sub-basin from 2001 to 2024, using ERA5‑Land, Terra/Aqua AOD and Landsat NDVI, and compared multiple linear regression with ANOVA against XGBoost explained with SHAP.

Key findings

  • Volumetric soil water and precipitation have the strongest linear influence on VCI; wind speed is not significant.
  • ANOVA identifies aerosol optical depth and surface pressure as the most significant drivers (P < 0.01).
  • SHAP shows solar radiation and air temperature contribute most to the XGBoost predictions.

Research areas

The questions our team investigates, whose answers feed straight into the platform's modules.

Agricultural drought from space

VCI and NDVI, long time series and their link to crop yield.

Hydrodynamic flood modelling

2D simulation of flood extent and depth across farmland.

Multivariate extremes & uncertainty

Compound flooding, joint analysis of drivers and confidence ranges on estimates.

Explainable machine learning

Models that show the contribution of each driver, not just a prediction (SHAP).

Spectral plant health & pests

Red-edge bands and anomaly detection for early stress detection.

Water resources & nature-based solutions

Optimal water allocation and assessing nature-based solutions for risk reduction.
