ArtaSat modules

Every module follows a clear path, from detecting the event to the final damage figure. Pick a module to see how it works.

Drought & yield loss

Crop detection, yield estimation and parcel-level damage

ArtaSat analyses satellite, climate and spatial data to monitor every parcel, then uses AI-based models to estimate drought-driven yield reduction and damage.

How it works

  1. 01
    Cropland detectionSeparate farmland and orchards from other land uses
  2. 02
    Crop-type mappingIdentify the crop grown in each parcel
  3. 03
    Yield estimationEstimate expected versus actual yield
  4. 04
    Parcel-level damageAssign a damage severity to every field
  5. 05
    Village roll-upDeliver results at village, district and province scale

Outputs

  • Crop-type map
  • Agricultural drought severity map
  • Yield loss per parcel
  • Village and province summary reports

Flood

Identify flood-exposed land and estimate damage severity

Satellite imagery, rainfall, terrain and land-use maps are combined with hydrodynamic modelling to delineate flooded areas, and machine-learning models estimate damage severity at parcel, village and district scale.

How it works

  1. 01
    Event detectionTrack intense rainfall, runoff and water-level changes
  2. 02
    Affected landIntersect the flood extent with parcels and crop types
  3. 03
    Severity assessmentInundation duration, growth stage and vegetation indices
  4. 04
    Damage estimateParcel-level loss, rolled up to village and province

Outputs

  • Flood extent mapping
  • Damaged-land map
  • Damage severity per field
  • Hydrodynamic modelling scenarios

Hail & untimely rainfall

Extreme-event analysis and losses from shifting rainfall patterns

Hail, intense rain, sudden frost and off-season rainfall can cut crop quality and yield within a short window. ArtaSat analyses the event, the crop's growth stage and vegetation change together to estimate damage severity at field scale.

How it works

  1. 01
    Event detectionHail, heavy rain, sudden frost or off-season rainfall
  2. 02
    Crop matchingAssess crop sensitivity at its current growth stage
  3. 03
    Field impactAnalyse changes in vegetation indices and crop health
  4. 04
    Loss calculationEstimate yield reduction and economic loss per parcel

Outputs

  • Event and intensity map
  • List of damaged parcels
  • Before/after comparison
  • SMS alerts to farmers

Water resources management

Optimising surface and groundwater use for each farm

ArtaSat analyses surface and groundwater availability against each farm's water demand, and its optimisation algorithm recommends a sound water-use pattern at field scale.

  2. 02

Pest remote sensing

Early detection of pest hotspots and damage estimation from space

Pests and diseases change a plant's spectral signature before damage is visible to the eye. This module analyses red-edge and near-infrared bands, vegetation-index time series and weather conditions to detect plant-health anomalies, separate them from water stress and map likely pest hotspots.

How it works

  1. 01
    Continuous spectral monitoringNDVI, NDRE and red-edge time series for every parcel
  2. 02
    Anomaly detectionSeparate abnormal health decline from the normal seasonal curve
  3. 03
    Stress attributionTell pest damage apart from drought and water stress using climate and phenology
  4. 04
    Hotspot & spread mappingLocate hotspots and follow how they spread over time
  5. 05
    Damage & targeted scoutingLoss estimates for insurers and suggested points for field inspection

Outputs

  • Likely pest-hotspot map
  • Early warnings to farmers
  • Pest-damage estimates for insurers
  • Priority points for field scouting
